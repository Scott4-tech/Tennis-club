About Scotty Tennis Club

Building a legacy of excellence since 2017

Our Club

Founded in 2017, Scotty Tennis Club was built on a simple passion: bringing people together through the love of tennis. What started as a small gathering of local tennis enthusiasts has grown into a thriving community for players of all ages and skill levels.

At Scotty Tennis Club, we believe tennis is more than just a sport—it’s a way to stay active, build lasting friendships, and challenge yourself. Whether you are picking up a racket for the very first time, looking to join a social league, or wanting to sharpen your competitive edge, our club provides a welcoming and supportive environment.

With dedicated coaching, well-maintained courts, and a calendar full of exciting tournaments and social events, we are proud to be your home on the court. Come join the Scotty Tennis family—we can’t wait to see you out there!

Our Mission

Our mission is to foster a love for tennis while promoting physical fitness, sportsmanship, and community engagement. We strive to create an inclusive environment where players of all backgrounds can come together to learn, compete, and grow both on and off the court.

Through our programs, we aim to develop not only skilled athletes but also confident individuals who carry the values of teamwork, perseverance, and respect into every aspect of their lives. At Scotty Tennis Club, we are committed to making a positive impact on our members and the wider community through the power of tennis.

Why Choose Scotty?

More than just a place to play, we are a place to belong.

Expert Coaching

Our professional coaching staff is dedicated to helping you improve your technique, strategy, and overall fitness, no matter your starting level.

Vibrant Community

From weekend BBQ mixers to competitive club ladders, our social events make it easy to find playing partners and make lasting friends.

Top-Tier Facilities

Meet the Coaches

A great club is built by great leadership. Our team of certified professional instructors brings decades of combined experience from collegiate to professional circuits.

We pride ourselves on personalized instruction. Whether we are breaking down the mechanics of a serve for a beginner or running high-intensity endurance drills for our advanced tournament players, our coaches are invested in your success.
